Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

PromptGallery respects your privacy. The app is designed to help you save, organize, search, and export your image-generation prompts while keeping your content under your control.

Information You Provide

PromptGallery stores the content you choose to save in the app, which may include:

  • Prompt text.
  • Creation date for each saved prompt.
  • Optional images and thumbnails attached to prompts.
  • Optional generated organization data, such as a short summary, visual style, prompt type, keywords, and the date that analysis was generated.

Apple Intelligence

When available on your device, PromptGallery may use Apple Intelligence through Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework to analyze prompt text and generate concise labels, styles, categories, and keywords for search and organization.

This analysis is based on the prompt text only. Attached images are not used for Apple Intelligence analysis.

PromptGallery does not send your prompts to the developer's servers for this analysis.

iCloud and CloudKit Sync

PromptGallery may use Apple's CloudKit and your private iCloud account to sync your saved prompts and related app data across your devices.

CloudKit data is stored in the user's private iCloud database. The developer does not operate a separate server for this data and does not use CloudKit to make your prompts publicly available.

Your use of iCloud and CloudKit is also subject to Apple's privacy policy and iCloud terms.

How Information Is Used

The information stored in PromptGallery is used to provide app features, including:

  • Displaying your saved prompts and attached media.
  • Searching prompts and generated organization data.
  • Filtering prompts by generated keywords.
  • Detecting duplicate prompts and validating stored media.
  • Exporting selected prompt data as a CSV file when you choose to share it.
  • Syncing your app data through your private iCloud account when CloudKit is available.

Data Collection

PromptGallery does not collect, sell, or share personal information with the developer.

The app does not use developer-operated analytics, advertising networks, tracking SDKs, or third-party servers to collect your prompts, images, or generated organization data.

Sharing and Export

PromptGallery includes sharing and export features that are controlled by you. When you export CSV data or share content using system share features, the destination and handling of that data depend on the app or service you choose.

Third-Party Services

PromptGallery relies on Apple system services and frameworks, including SwiftData, CloudKit, Photos picker, and Apple Intelligence where available. The app does not share your saved prompts or images with non-Apple third-party services.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
